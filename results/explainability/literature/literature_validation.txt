✓  Salience Network
   Jaccard similarity : 0.4286
   Network regions    : Motor_Premotor, Insula, Cingulate, Subcortical
   Matched by model   : Motor_Premotor, Insula, Cingulate
   ASD finding        : Reduced salience network connectivity; impaired interoceptive
     awareness via anterior insula; hypo-connectivity between insula
     and ACC.
   Key refs           : Uddin et al. (2013), JAMA Psychiatry; Paulus & Stein (2006), Trends Cogn Sci; Sridharan et al. (2008), PNAS

   Jaccard similarity : 0.0000
